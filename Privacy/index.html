Tengely privacy policy

Updated November 17, 2026. Consent version: 2026-11-17-v1.

Data on your device

Tengely stores transactions, categories, goals, manual savings, chat history, settings and category decisions on your iPhone. The original PDF is read on the device and is not uploaded in full to AI. Local categorization and analytics remain available without AI.

AI requires your permission

The app does not send data to AI before you consent. After consent, dates, amounts, merchants or counterparties, payment descriptions and categories are sent through our Google Firebase / Google Cloud server to OpenRouter, Inc. and then NVIDIA. Chat also sends your message, part of the history and relevant transactions; missions also send your goal and recent transactions. Descriptions may contain names and other personal information. The purposes are categorization, error review, answers and missions.

Third-party services

Google provides the Firebase server infrastructure. OpenRouter routes requests to NVIDIA, which runs the Nemotron model. These organizations handle retention and use of the transmitted data under their own policies and terms. We do not promise that providers will not retain data or use it to improve models. Recipients cannot be changed automatically without updating consent in the app.

Online features and limits

Battles are a separate online feature: Firebase stores the room, display name, savings amount, goal and progress, which other participants can see. This is independent of AI being enabled. AI chat uses an anonymous Firebase profile; its refresh token is stored in Keychain. The server stores a hash of the profile ID, the day and token usage to enforce the daily limit. Counters are retained until an administrator deletes them; automatic deletion is not yet configured.

Retention and deletion

Tengely does not create a separate server database of your statements and messages: they are transmitted for processing. Infrastructure and AI providers may keep technical logs and apply their own retention periods. Delete local transactions in Analytics and chat history through the chat menu. Clearing the app does not automatically delete data already shared with third parties. Contact the developer about processing and deletion of server data.

Your choice

You can decline AI or withdraw permission in Settings → Privacy → AI processing. Withdrawing permission stops new submissions, cancels pending client AI requests and keeps local features available. Data already sent to the server or provider cannot be retrieved by cancelling a request. Enabling AI again requires explicit permission.
